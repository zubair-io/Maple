# test_0003 baseline diagnosis — signature

Fixture: test_0003 = **Canon EOS 5DS R, CR2**, `--profile neutral`. Diagnosed 2026-06-04 with the new per-zone/per-hue instrument (`ZONES=1`) + `diff_heatmap.py` + GitHub history. Gates the Component-2 fix in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-06-03-perzone-deltae-harness-and-baseline-hardening.md`.

## Verdict (two layers)

**Layer 1 — test_0003: two residual terms, one dominant, one untested.** The bundle-matrix / WB path is sound (neutrals chromatically tight). The residual splits into (a) a **dominant tonal term** — the AgX-vs-ACR view-transform gap, which the `--profile neutral` harness excludes by design and which is Feature-2 (look/Auto-Profile) territory; and (b) a **real, hue-dependent chroma term** — the missing per-camera 2D HueSatMap, now **tested and confirmed the dominant fixable lever** (the 5DS R's 2D HSM drops mean 6.63→5.88, under budget — see the experiment section). It misses budget marginally (mean 6.63 vs 6.26, p95 10.94 vs 10.37; **no bias or max breach**).

**Layer 2 — the failure is not fixture-specific.** Under the current `--profile neutral` path, **12 of 17 baselines breach their budgets**, and the gate is **blind in CI** (RAWs *and* references are gitignored → the harness skip-passes per CLAUDE.md). Heterogeneous causes (below). Whether this is a *known, pending-re-baseline* state or an unnoticed break is the user's call — surfaced, not assumed.

## Layer 1 — evidence (test_0003)

- **Profile path:** `maple-cli inspect` → "color matrices: 0 illuminants" (the CR2 carries no embedded DCP) → color comes from the **bundle's 5DS R matrices**. Sound: the neutral hue bucket is the tightest class (4.57), and that 4.57 is mostly the *shared tonal bias* → no meaningful chromatic neutral error → not a matrix/profile-target mismatch.
- **Tonal term (dominant).** Zones (binned on reference L\*): shadow 5.37 (+.046/+.029/+.017), **mid 7.85 (+.070/+.064/+.063)**, highlight 5.55 (−.058/−.061/−.054). R/G/B bias move **together** within each zone → a brightness/contrast difference (mid lift + highlight compression), not a cast. This is the AgX tone curve vs ACR's rendering intent — the ~4.5 floor that sits under every pixel.
- **Chroma term (untested HSM lever).** The per-hue a\*/b\* shifts **rotate systematically with hue** — not flat, not a single spike:

  | hue bin | a\* shift | b\* shift |  | hue bin | a\* shift | b\* shift |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | [0,30] | −5.0 | −1.4 | | [180,210] | +3.7 | +0.9 |
  | [60,90] | −1.7 | −5.4 | | [240,270] | +1.7 | +0.9 |
  | [90,120] | +1.6 | −7.5 | | [270,300] | −0.7 | +6.9 |
  | [120,150] | +4.2 | −4.3 | | [330,360] | −3.9 | +1.1 |

  A shift vector that *rotates with hue* (±5–7) is the textbook **missing-2D-HueSatMap signature** — HSM corrects exactly this hue-dependent chroma error, and it presents as a distributed per-hue deviation, **not** a single-hue blowout. Mean ΔE is hue-flat at 6.2–8.5 only because the tonal floor masks it. Closing these shifts pulls chromatic pixels toward the ~4.5 floor and moves the global mean under 6.26 — **confirmed by the HSM experiment below** (mean 6.63→5.88, per-hue shift −80%).
- **Spatial (heatmap quadrants):** BL 7.35 / BR 7.04 vs TL 6.11 / TR 6.01 → error concentrates in the lower (mid-tone foreground) half. Artifacts: `~/Desktop/maple-color-tests/test_0003/`.

## HSM experiment result (2026-06-04) — lever validated

Sourced `Canon EOS 5DS R Adobe Standard v2.dcp` (HSM dims **90×25×1 — 2D**, no #825 value axis; the value-dependence is in its 36×8×16 LookTable, which Maple drops) from the local Adobe install, built a scoped bundle with `convert_dcps.py --include-hsm`, swapped it in, recompiled, re-rendered test_0003 baseline `--profile neutral`:

| metric | no-HSM | +HSM | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| global mean ΔE | 6.63 | **5.88** | −0.75 (**under the 6.26 budget**) |
| global p95 | 10.94 | 10.44 | −0.50 (p95 budget 10.37 — still +0.07 over) |
| zone mid | 7.85 | 6.55 | −1.30 |
| zone highlight | 5.55 | 6.18 | **+0.63 (worse)** |
| neutral bucket | 4.57 | 4.56 | ~0 (HSM correctly leaves neutrals) |
| mean per-hue \|a\*\|+\|b\*\| | 6.23 | **1.25** | **−80%** |

**The 2D HSM lever is validated** — it collapses the hue-dependent chroma error by 80% and pulls the mean under budget, confirming term (b) was the missing HueSatMap. **Two blemishes** (characterized in the next section): the blue [240–270°] bin regressed (ΔE 7.89→10.26) and the highlight zone rose +0.63 — both are HSM **sat-scale chroma overshoots** (over-*saturation*), an #825 apply-math issue, *not* the value-axis problem the RFC predicted. Restored to the matrices-only bundle after measuring; nothing committed to the pipeline.

## HSM blemish investigation (2026-06-04)

Characterized from the saved candidate PNGs (no recompile). Net: **42% of pixels improve >1 ΔE, 13% worsen** — the regression is concentrated, and **both blemishes are one mechanism: the HSM sat-scale over-boosts chroma.**

- **Highlights — over-SATURATION (the RFC had the sign backwards here).** Mean chroma C\* in the highlight zone: reference 9.20, no-HSM **7.15** (AgX's path-to-white left highlights *under*-saturated), +HSM **14.45** (overshoot, +5.25 *past* the reference). The HSM's satScale > 1 adds back the saturation AgX removed — but too much.
- **Blue [240,270] — a b\* overshoot.** Reference b\* −10.82; no-HSM −9.89 (already close); +HSM **−19.57** (≈2× too blue). a\* is fine (−1.58 vs −1.94) and the hue doesn't rotate — a pure sat-scale over-push along b\*.

**Mechanism:** the Adobe HSM sat-scale is calibrated to sit under Adobe's tone curve; applied under **AgX** (different highlight rendering), the saturation lands too high. Same "Adobe aesthetic layer ↔ AgX rendering-intent mismatch" #425 cited when dropping PLT/PTC — now in the HSM sat-scale. It is **#825 apply-math**, and the fix is the *opposite* of the RFC's value-collapse: the 2D sat-scale must become **value-aware** (attenuated in highlights to match AgX), not value-collapsed. The hue-shift component is sound — it drove the −80% per-hue win; only the sat-scale overshoots. Regression map: `~/Desktop/maple-color-tests/test_0003/hsm_regression_map.png` (red = HSM worse, blue = better).

**Spike confirmation (2026-06-04).** Added an env-tunable value-aware sat-scale taper to `hsm::apply` (`eff_ss = 1 + (ss-1)·(1-smoothstep(lo,hi,V))`, V = scene-linear HSV value), swept thresholds. Scene-linear V is low (p50=0.076, p90=0.216, p99=0.753) since HSM runs pre-AgX/pre-auto-exposure.

| variant | mean | highlight | blue [240,270] |
|---|---|---|---|
| no-HSM | 6.63 | 5.55 | 7.89 |
| +HSM (no atten) | 5.88 | 6.18 | 10.26 |
| **+HSM +atten (0.10,0.40)** | **3.91** | **5.12** | **6.46** |

Value-aware attenuation **fixes both blemishes** (both now better than no-HSM) and drops the mean to **3.91** — the sat-scale was over-boosting broadly under AgX, not just in the two flagged spots. **Direction conclusively proven.** Caveat: thresholds are test_0003-tuned in scene-linear V; a production #825 fix needs an exposure-robust value reference (e.g. normalize against diffuse white, or apply post-auto-exposure). Spike reverted; nothing committed.

## Why the budget reads as a failure (the tonal-floor half)

`test_0003.baseline.mean` budget over time (GitHub history): 12.7 (May-1 seed, rawler-CM) → 9.2 (`eef7e7b9`, bundled DCP) → **6.26** at `1c28350e` — *"feat(view): DisplayLookCurve — empirical 1D LUT as default Look."* The harness pins `--profile neutral` and was unchanged, so the budget tightened **because the neutral render picked up the new default Look**. **#443 then retired DisplayLookCurve**: `look::apply` is now an empty no-op for both variants — [view/look.rs:122](src/raw-pipeline/raw-core/src/view/look.rs), pinned by `apply_is_no_op_for_both_variants`. So the current neutral render is pure AgX; the tonal correction the 6.26 budget captured is gone. The reopened gap for test_0003 is small (0.37). *Caveat:* the `1c28350e` drop also overlaps a crop re-baseline (`#375`), so it's not all attributable to the look.

## Layer 2 — blast radius (measured, current code vs current budgets)

Full `--profile neutral` sweep — **12/17 baselines breach.** Heterogeneous (not one root cause):

| pattern | fixtures | note |
|---|---|---|
| marginal (mean/p95 by a hair) | test_0003, test_0011, test_0007 | look-era budget drift + AgX floor |
| single-pixel outlier only | **test_0006** (max 42.96>32.86; mean 37% *under* budget), test_0015 (bias_R over by 0.001) | a hot-pixel/clip — not tonal |
| large, **positive** bias | test_0014 (+0.19), test_0001 (+0.14), test_0012 (+0.14), test_0005 (+0.11), test_0009 (+0.086) | Maple too bright/warm |
| large, **negative** bias | test_0002 (−0.21, mean 12.89 vs 2.20), test_0013 (−0.05) | Maple too dark/cool |

**The bias signs are inconsistent** (+0.19 vs −0.21) → *not* a single uniform "look removed" shift. It's budgets drifted out of alignment with the post-pivot pipeline **plus** genuine per-fixture deltas, masked by the CI skip-pass.
