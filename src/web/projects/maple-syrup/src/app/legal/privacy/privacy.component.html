This Privacy Policy explains what data Maple — the RAW photo editor published by Just Maple ("Just Maple", "we", "us") — collects and how it's used, across the hosted web app at maple-editor.com ("Maple Hosted"), the self-hosted application you may run on your own infrastructure ("Self Hosted"), and the native macOS, iOS, and iPadOS apps.

Your photos stay with you

Maple's editing pipeline runs on-device. In Maple Hosted, your photos are decoded and rendered locally in your browser and cached on your own device (in your browser's local storage); they are not uploaded to our servers as part of normal editing. On macOS, iOS, and iPadOS, photos never leave your device unless you explicitly turn on a cloud feature or connect the app to a Self Hosted server you control. If you run Self Hosted, your photo library, metadata, and edit history are stored on infrastructure you operate. Optional integrations can send data to services you connect, as described below.

Optional Google Drive backup

Self Hosted can back up selected photo libraries to a visible Maple folder in your Google Drive. Backup includes indexed originals, paired XMP sidecars, recorded rendered companions, and metadata needed to recover them, including Maple Trash state. Photos and backup files transfer directly between your Self Hosted server and Google; they do not pass through Maple's connection proxy. Google processes data stored in Drive under its own terms and privacy policy.

Maple requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, which provides per-file access to files created or opened with the application. It does not request access to your entire Drive. Maple additionally checks that backup operations concern its owned backup folder and artifacts. Google enforces per-file access, rather than a permission restricted to one folder. Your server reads the connected account's name and email to identify that connection.

With Maple's Google application, a connection proxy hosted on Cloudflare temporarily processes authorization codes, PKCE verifiers, access tokens, and refresh tokens to connect your account and renew authorization. The proxy also processes the server callback address and signed connection metadata. It does not store these credentials or provide a photo download endpoint. Because it processes tokens, the proxy has technical access to the Drive authorization they permit during processing. Cloudflare processes these requests as our hosting provider. Your server stores the refresh token and renewal grant encrypted in its local database; protect that server and its database backups.

If you bring your own Google client ID and secret, your server exchanges and renews tokens directly with Google and stores the client secret and refresh token encrypted locally. Choosing Maple's optional callback relay in this mode sends the initial authorization code, signed state, and callback address through the relay, but token exchange and renewal remain direct. Access tokens are held in server memory. Connection pages do not store handoff credentials in browser local storage or IndexedDB.

Google Drive data is used to connect your account and provide backup and recovery. Just Maple does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Maple's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Optional AI-assisted features

Some Self Hosted deployments can enable optional AI-assisted features, such as automatic photo descriptions and text recognition, which send image data to a vision model endpoint you configure. This only happens when an operator explicitly turns the feature on in Settings; it is off by default and Just Maple does not operate or receive data from these endpoints.

Diagnostics

Self Hosted deployments can optionally enable OpenTelemetry-based diagnostics, which send performance traces and error logs to an endpoint the operator configures. This is off by default and stays dormant until an operator sets it up; Just Maple does not receive this data. Maple Hosted and the native apps do not send diagnostics directly to Just Maple. On Apple platforms, Apple may provide crash reports when a user has chosen to share diagnostics with app developers; Apple controls that collection and sharing under its own privacy terms.

Account information

If a Self Hosted deployment has user accounts enabled, the operator's own server stores the account details (such as name and email) needed to sign in and manage access. That data is controlled by the operator running the server, not by Just Maple.

Cookies and local storage

Maple Hosted uses browser local storage and IndexedDB to keep your library, cached previews, and edits available between visits. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can clear Maple Hosted's local data at any time from your browser's site settings. Just Maple does not retain a separate hosted copy of your photo library. If backup is enabled, moving an item to Maple Trash retains its backup for restore. Explicit permanent deletion or intentional Trash retention expiry schedules deletion from configured backup destinations; offline or disconnected destinations can leave that deletion pending. A missing source file alone does not authorize deleting its backup.

Disconnecting Drive clears the server's stored authorization credentials and stops authenticated operations; it does not delete existing backups or revoke the Google account's application grant. You can revoke that grant in your Google Account settings. Revoking Maple's shared application can affect other Maple connections using that account. Removing a backup destination leaves its remote files behind and ends future deletion tracking. Review retained backups in Drive and preserve your server's database while deletion is pending. To ask about data an operator's Self Hosted deployment may hold on your behalf, contact that operator directly.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
