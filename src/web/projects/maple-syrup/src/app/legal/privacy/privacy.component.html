This Privacy Policy explains what data Maple — the RAW photo editor published by Just Maple ("Just Maple", "we", "us") — collects and how it's used, across the hosted web app at maple-editor.com ("Maple Hosted"), the self-hosted application you may run on your own infrastructure ("Self Hosted"), and the native macOS, iOS, and iPadOS apps.

Your photos stay with you

Maple's editing pipeline runs on-device. In Maple Hosted, your photos are decoded and rendered locally in your browser and cached on your own device (in your browser's local storage); they are not uploaded to our servers as part of normal editing. On macOS, iOS, and iPadOS, photos never leave your device unless you explicitly turn on a cloud feature or connect the app to a Self Hosted server you control. If you run Self Hosted, your photo library, metadata, and edit history are stored on infrastructure you operate, and Just Maple has no access to them.

Optional AI-assisted features

Some Self Hosted deployments can enable optional AI-assisted features, such as automatic photo descriptions and text recognition, which send image data to a vision model endpoint you configure. This only happens when an operator explicitly turns the feature on in Settings; it is off by default and Just Maple does not operate or receive data from these endpoints.

Diagnostics

Self Hosted deployments can optionally enable OpenTelemetry-based diagnostics, which send performance traces and error logs to an endpoint the operator configures. This is off by default and stays dormant until an operator sets it up; Just Maple does not receive this data. Maple Hosted and the native apps report crashes and errors only to help us fix bugs, and never include your photos or file names.

Account information

If a Self Hosted deployment has user accounts enabled, the operator's own server stores the account details (such as name and email) needed to sign in and manage access. That data is controlled by the operator running the server, not by Just Maple.

Cookies and local storage

Maple Hosted uses browser local storage and IndexedDB to keep your library, cached previews, and edits available between visits. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can clear Maple Hosted's local data at any time from your browser's site settings. Because your photos and library live on your own device or your own Self Hosted server, deleting a file there is how you delete it — Just Maple doesn't retain a separate copy. To ask about data an operator's Self Hosted deployment may hold on your behalf, contact that operator directly.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above.
