Contact support

Email help@justmaple.app for technical support, general feedback, or feature requests.

What to include

Tell us which platform and Maple version you're using, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and the steps that reproduce the issue. You don't need to send an original photo unless we ask for one and you choose to share it.

Protecting your originals

Maple is non-destructive: edits are stored in XMP sidecars and original photos are never modified. Keep backups of your originals and sidecars before troubleshooting storage, migration, or self-hosted deployment issues.
