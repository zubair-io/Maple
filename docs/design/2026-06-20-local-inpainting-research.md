# Local AI Inpainting — Code-Research Findings & Design Recommendation (Final)

> Status: final, post-verification. Three adversarial verifiers checked the riskiest claims against the code. All three returned **partial** — the architecture holds, but three load-bearing claims were qualified and are corrected below. See §8 for the confirmed-vs-qualified ledger with file:line evidence.
>
> Produced 2026-06-20 by a 15-agent research workflow (6 codebase readers, 4 external SOTA-research agents, synthesis, 3 adversarial verifiers, reconciliation).

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Ship LaMa, not diffusion, as the v1 engine.** LaMa (`big-lama`, Apache-2.0, ~27M params, ~50–100MB exported, sub-second on M-series at 1–2k px, deterministic, prompt-free image+mask) is the right primary inpainter for a RAW editor — the highest-quality *deterministic* eraser and the one that fits Maple's "XMP is the contract" invariant cleanly. Diffusion (SDXL-Fill / FLUX-Fill) is a heavier, opt-in Phase 2 — only if LaMa's fills are insufficient on busy backgrounds — and FLUX is disqualified for v1 by its non-commercial license.

2. **The one decision the whole thing hangs on: composite the inverse-tone-mapped patch into the scene-linear Rec.2020 buffer immediately before the view transform.** That boundary is real and the design lands on it correctly: the develop chain enters `SceneLinearRec2020` right after DCP (`develop/mod.rs:332`) and stays there through every user stage; AgX is the last thing and is gated behind `if !skip_agx` in the FFI tail (`scene_linear_chain.rs:229`), with a dedicated scene-linear injection FFI (`render_from_scene_linear`, `render/mod.rs:320`) that runs *only* the view tail. Compositing a synthetic-raw patch there grades it through the *exact same* AgX + Oklab gamut-compress + sRGB-encode + Auto Profile tail as real sensor pixels. Everything else (model choice, runtime, mask UX) is replaceable; this placement is the load-bearing architectural commitment, and it is sound.

3. **AgX inversion is viable for in-gamut midtone content only — and that is the honest bar, not "invertible enough."** *(Corrected — verifier downgraded this from the draft's stronger claim.)* The cleanly reversible stages are real: inset/outset matrices are exact mutual inverses, the sigmoid is a strictly-monotone LUT invertible in its interior, and the display matrices + sRGB OETF are analytically invertible. But four stages are genuinely lossy or non-invertible and the inverse must be *constrained*, not assumed accurate:
   - **AgX is non-injective at the extremes.** `log_encode` clamps to `[MIN_EV, MAX_EV] = [-10, +6.5]` (`agx.rs:100-105`, `agx_coeffs.rs:22-23`); everything below the toe collapses to one display value and everything above the shoulder collapses too (`negative_inputs_clamp_to_toe`, `agx.rs:315`). Worse than "slightly lossy": 16.5 stops compressed into a 512-entry LUT then into 8 bits is **severely ill-conditioned at the extremes** — 1 LSB near mid-gray ≈ 1.8% relative, but 1 LSB near the flat shoulder/toe can map to **more than a full multiplicative stop** of scene-linear error. Reconstructions are coherent in midtones and *unstable* in deep shadow / bright highlight.
   - **Oklab gamut compression is genuinely many-to-one** (`oklab_gamut.rs:43`, `agx_hue_restoration.rs:95`): every out-of-`[0,1]³` chroma along a ray is collapsed onto the gamut wall by bisection. Boundary-saturated content has *lost* the chroma needed to recover scene values; the inverse can only assume in-gamut. Tolerable for skin/sky/foliage (rarely on the boundary), but a hard bound the design must respect — do not rely on boundary reconstruction.
   - **Auto Profile is two artifacts, only one invertible.** The per-image, per-channel #550 display curve is monotone and 1D-LUT-invertible (`curve.rs:247`, `fit_display.rs:259-264`). But a **separate residual 3D ColorLut** is also fit and applied (`lut.rs:197/281`, `render/mod.rs:154`) — a smoothed, clamped, trilinear warp from sparse pairs, **not closed-form invertible** and many-to-one near gamut corners. The legacy `apply.rs` path additionally carries cross-channel matrix + Oklab corrections that are **not per-channel invertible at all** (`apply.rs:45-110`). The inverse must invert **only** through the monotone #550 per-channel curve, with the 3D LUT disabled (`MAPLE_DISABLE_AUTO_LUT` / strength 0, `lut.rs:143-162`) and the legacy Oklab-correction path guaranteed out of play.
   - **Per-image snapshot is mandatory.** The Auto Profile curve is fit against the embedded camera JPEG, which does **not** contain the patch (`fit_display.rs:67-265`). To grade coherently the patch must be inverted through, then re-graded forward through, the *same cached `(curve, lut)` pair for that exact image*. The compositor must capture and re-apply that pair, and re-fit invalidates on the env flags.

   The draft also said "Rec.2020→P3"; the **default encode is Rec.2020→sRGB** (`TargetPrimaries::Srgb`, `encode.rs:28-34`) — P3 is a separate, also-invertible primary rotation. Corrected in §3b. **Net: the claim is true under those five constraints (midtone-only inversion, #550 curve only, per-image snapshot, bounded shadow/highlight error, never trust boundary chroma) and false as an unqualified statement.** And there is **no inverse in the codebase today** — it is entirely new code that must also match the `raw-gpu`/WGSL live path bit-for-bit (`raw-gpu/src/agx.rs:43` shares `agx_lut.bin`).

4. **Reuse the panorama ML *pattern* — but it is new plumbing, not a drop-in (~50% reuse, not 80%).** *(Corrected — verifier downgraded "wholesale reuse.")* The building blocks are real and good prior art: a vendored `ort` with a genuine CoreML EP (`vendor/ort/.../coreml.rs`, registered live in `lightglue.rs:161-169`), and a SHA-256-pinned download/verify/atomic-install provisioner (`models.rs`, `PanoProvisioner.swift`, integrity re-verify in `PanoProvisioner+Integrity.swift`). But "reuse rather than build from scratch" oversimplifies in four ways the design must budget for:
   - **The ML stack lives in `maple-pano`, not `raw-core`.** `raw-core` has *zero* ort/onnx dependency, yet the scene-linear composite must happen inside `raw-core`. Reuse means either adding a new `raw-core ↔ ML` architectural edge or running inference in a sibling crate and plumbing pixels back — neither is free.
   - **The FFI is a single monolithic `maple_pano_stitch` call**, not a general inference API (`raw-ffi/src/pano_apple.rs`). There is no `maple_infer` / `maple_segment`. New FFI is required.
   - **The manifest is a fixed two-model struct** (`aliked` + `lightglue`), mirrored as a hardcoded Swift array (`PanoProvisionManifest.swift:55-70`). It must be generalized to a keyed/`Vec` registry on both sides.
   - **There are already three separate ML/provision stacks** — Rust `ort` (pano), API `onnxruntime-node` for faces (`api/src/index.ts:30`), and a third antelopev2 provisioner (`face-models-bootstrap.ts`). The codebase pattern is "each ML feature ships its own runtime + provisioner," which is evidence *for* copyability and *against* one shared system to plug into.
   - **CoreML-EP reuse is unvalidated for new op-sets.** The EP is registered iOS-only, macOS deliberately stays CPU-ORT, and the code itself flags ANE speedup as "unvalidated on-device — pending M6-E" (`lightglue.rs:110`). Op coverage was hand-checked for LightGlue/ALIKED; a LaMa/SAM-class op set is different and must be re-validated. iOS links ORT 1.22 static while macOS dlopens 1.23.2 — version skew the new models must tolerate.

   So: copy the **provisioning + EP-registration pattern** wholesale; expect to **build new plumbing** (raw-core↔ML edge, a generic inference FFI, a multi-model manifest, per-model session wrappers). Keep ORT in a native-only sibling crate (the `maple-pano` precedent) so the WASM build stays model-free.

5. **Bake-once is correct, but for LaMa it's a recomputable performance cache, not a correctness crutch.** On-device inference is provably non-reproducible across chips/OS/EPs (PyTorch, HF Diffusers, and Apple's own Core ML docs all decline to guarantee same-seed reproducibility; fp16 non-associativity + ANE-vs-GPU placement seal it; Maple's own pano path runs CPU-ORT on macOS and CoreML/ANE on iOS with iOS output explicitly unvalidated). For diffusion the bake is *mandatory* (unreproducible result). For deterministic LaMa the patch is a pure function of `(source, mask, model_version)` — cache it for latency but always regenerable from the XMP-recorded mask. Either way the cached patch is fp16 scene-linear Rec.2020 in `.maple/inpaint/`, referenced from XMP by hash.

6. **Apple-only v1; defer web explicitly.** Web has zero ML runtime, `raw-wasm` has no `ort` feature, the team already documented ORT-on-WASM as a future milestone (`maple-pano/Cargo.toml:28-31`), and a diffusion/LaMa model on top of a 100MP RAW would blow the ~4GB WASM heap. The shared *pixel math* (inverse-AgX + composite) lives in raw-core and both platforms consume it; only inference is platform-specific. **Note:** "reuse rather than build from scratch" is true for the Apple-native path and **false for web** — web has no ONNX runtime at all and would be built fresh.

## 2. Recommended model stack

### Inpainter

**Product framing — "Remove/Heal" ≠ "Generative Fill".** Ship the feed-forward erasers (LaMa/MI-GAN) as a **Remove** tool: an eraser/healer that reconstructs plausible background, deterministic, regenerable from the sidecar. Reserve "**Generative Fill**" as a *separate, later, explicitly-labeled* feature for diffusion (object synthesis), with its own opt-in and the mandatory non-reproducible bake. Keeping v1 framed as **Remove** sets the right user expectation (no Photoshop-Generative-Fill prompt-to-synthesize) and matches the engineering split (Phase 2 vs Phase 4).

**Primary: LaMa (`advimman/lama`, big-lama config), Apache-2.0.** FFC (Fast-Fourier-Convolution) architecture, ~27M params, ~200MB checkpoint / ~50–100MB exported, sub-second on M-series GPU at 1–2k px, generalizes to ~2k resolutions it never trained on. Strongest FID among feed-forward erasers (RORD FID 49.2 vs MAT 86.3). Pure image+mask, no text prompt — matching a RAW editor with no prompt UX. Exports cleanly to ONNX (IOPaint ships it) and Core ML; the FFT/FFC layers need attention on export but are proven. **Deterministic**, so it rides Maple's non-destructive model without persisting an unreproducible result. **CoreML-EP caveat:** LaMa's FFT/FFC op set has *not* been validated against the existing CoreML EP (which was hand-checked for ALIKED/LightGlue only); expect graph partition / CPU fallback until validated — budget that as a Phase-2 task, not an assumption.

**Deterministic fallback / mobile-first: MI-GAN (`Picsart-AI-Research/MI-GAN`), MIT.** ~3–4M params (512 model), tens of MB, real-time even on phones, fully-convolutional → cleanest Core ML conversion, ships a pre-converted ONNX pipeline with pre/post-processing. Use it as the iOS/iPad default where LaMa's FFT layers or memory budget bite; fall back to LaMa on Mac for top quality. Both are deterministic and share the same cache + inverse-tone-map plumbing.

**Diffusion (Phase 2, opt-in only): SD 1.5 inpainting via Apple `ml-stable-diffusion`, 6-bit palettization.** The *only* diffusion inpainter with a first-class Core ML path (~7–11s on M1/M2, ANE via `SPLIT_EINSUM`, inpaint = base UNet + 5 input channels). **Avoid FLUX.1 Fill** despite best-in-class quality: non-commercial license (hard blocker) and 12B/~24GB/~50s+ (too heavy for the "few seconds" bar).

### Segmentation

**Auto "remove people/objects" — lead with Apple Vision (zero download).** `VNGeneratePersonInstanceMaskRequest` for per-instance people (remove *one* person, not all) and `VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest` for class-agnostic salient objects (the engine behind system subject-lifting). Both ANE-accelerated, ship in iOS 17 / macOS 14, zero model bytes to provision. Accept the known 4-person cap and slightly softer hair edges — minor for background cleanup. The repo has **zero** segmentation today (no `import Vision`, no `VNGenerate*`, no CoreML in `src/apple`), so this is net-new wiring but avoids shipping a model.

**Escalation / web auto path — BiRefNet (MIT) as ONNX.** SOTA-class hair/edge mattes, commercially clean. The only auto matter on web (no Vision there). Do **not** default to RMBG-2.0 (CC-BY-NC, paid commercial) or RVM (GPL-3.0).

**Manual selection.** Pure paint/lasso needs **no model** — a user-drawn raster mask. Ship that first. Smart click-to-select is a scoped Phase-2 add with one promptable model: **EdgeSAM** (9.6M, real Core ML encoder+decoder, 30+ FPS iPhone 14) *if its NTU S-Lab license clears for commercial use*; otherwise **MobileSAM** (Apache, ~40MB) on both Apple and web.

### Runtime

**ORT + CoreML EP, copying the existing pattern — for segmentation and the LaMa/MI-GAN inpainter.** Both are CNN/transformer feed-forward, deterministic, and slot into the session/EP-registration pattern Maple already runs (`lightglue.rs` registers CoreML EP ahead of CPU with graceful fallback). Adding a model is a new manifest entry + session wrapper — but, per §1.4 and the verifier, that pattern lives in `maple-pano`, is exposed only via a monolithic stitch FFI, and the EP's op coverage for these specific models is unvalidated. Treat CoreML acceleration as a *goal to verify per model*, not a given; CPU-ORT is the guaranteed-correct floor (as macOS already does for pano).

Do **not** force diffusion through CoreML EP (graph partition → silent CPU fallback, no published fast benchmark). If diffusion becomes a hard requirement, add native Core ML (`.mlpackage` via `ml-stable-diffusion`) as a *second, isolated* runtime behind the same provisioner. Do **not** adopt MLX unless FLUX-Fill specifically becomes the committed target.

## 3. Answers to every open question

### a. Scene-linear working buffer: location, bit depth, gamut, clean composite?

The type is **`crate::image::Image { width: u32, height: u32, pixels: Vec<[f32;3]>, space: ColorSpace }`** (`image.rs:47-53`, verified). **f32, 3-channel RGB, packed row-major, NO alpha.** fp16 is transport-only at the FFI boundary (`fp16.rs`, `CIImage.RGBAh`), unpacked to f32 on entry to the per-tick chain. Gamut is **linear Rec.2020 D65, unbounded** (`image.rs:17-19`, verified: "scene-referred linear Rec.2020 D65, f32, **unbounded**. Main working space").

A synthetic patch composites cleanly **as pixel data** — same f32 linear Rec.2020 RGB, and the timing/colorspace seam is confirmed real (verifier: "a real, well-defined pre-view-transform insertion point on a scene-linear f32 buffer genuinely exists"). **But the existing local-adjustment/mask machinery cannot carry it** *(corrected — verifier refuted the "reuse the mask machinery" half outright)*:

- **No raster mask exists.** `Mask` is a closed two-variant *parametric* enum — `Linear`/`Radial` only (`mod.rs:107-130`); `mask::evaluate` is a pure analytic function of `(mask, x_norm, y_norm)` with no allocations (`mask.rs:1-21`). Brush masks are explicitly *rejected* as malformed today (`wire.rs:277`), and the module doc defers brush/AI-subject masks to follow-up tickets (`mod.rs:11`).
- **No pixel-replace op.** `PartialAdjustments` carry only scalar grading controls, and `apply_pixel` only *multiplies* existing pixels by `exp2(w*ev)` (`local_adjustments/mod.rs:76-98`) — it modulates sensor data, it cannot *replace* a pixel with synthetic RGB. `out = mix(scene, patch, mask)` is not expressible there.
- **Resolution model is incompatible.** Masks are deliberately resolution-agnostic normalized coords so "the same XMP renders identically against full-res and downsampled buffers" (`mod.rs:24-27`). A fixed-resolution raster patch must instead be resampled/aligned to every buffer size (full develop, early-downsampled viewport, per-tick cached, padded zoom tiles) — which parametric masks get for free and a baked raster does not.

**Conclusion (unchanged from draft, now verifier-confirmed): you need a genuinely new construct.** Reuse the *buffer type* (`Image` / `SceneLinearRec2020` is a sufficient composite target); do **not** reuse the mask/adjustment machinery. The new stage does `out = lerp(orig, patch, mask_w)` over `Vec<[f32;3]>` with a *new* per-pixel raster mask representation and a *new* patch-pixel carrier. Coverage rides a **separate mask buffer**, not the pixel buffer (there is no alpha lane in `Image` — alpha exists only as a constant-1.0 lane in the packed RGBA FFI transport).

### b. AgX inverse: clean or approximate; required accuracy?

**Mostly analytically invertible in the interior; non-invertible at gamut walls and ill-conditioned at the AgX extremes.** *(Corrected for precision per verifier.)* Per-stage:

- **Inset/outset matrices:** exact mutual inverses, asserted `INSET @ OUTSET = I` to 1e-5 (`agx.rs:242-267`).
- **Sigmoid:** strictly-monotone 512-entry LUT (`agx.rs:184-195`) → reverse-LUT inverse exists *in its interior*. The ratio-preserving form means only per-pixel intensity needs inverting; hue/chroma direction survives (`agx_hue_restoration.rs:64-82`). **But:** the LUT compresses a clamped `[-10, +6.5]` EV domain (`agx.rs:100-105`), so the inverse is **severely ill-conditioned near the toe/shoulder** — sub-LSB display steps map to >1-stop scene-linear steps. Treat shadow/highlight reconstruction as *bounded-error*, not accurate; clamp the inverse domain and linear-splice the shoulder, and accept that near-clipped speculars are hallucinated, not recovered.
- **Oklab gamut compression (runs twice):** `agx.rs:144` + inside `rec2020_to_srgb` at `encode.rs:88`. Genuinely many-to-one — out-of-gamut chroma is collapsed onto the unit-cube wall by bisection and the excess is discarded (`oklab_gamut.rs:43-59`). In-gamut content takes a byte-identical pass-through, so this is lossy *only* for saturated/boundary content — but the inverse must **assume in-gamut and never rely on boundary chroma**.
- **Display encode:** the primary matrices and the sRGB OETF are exactly invertible (`encode.rs:82-160`). **Default target is Rec.2020→sRGB** (`TargetPrimaries::Srgb`, `encode.rs:28-34`), *not* P3 as the draft stated; P3 is a separate, also-exactly-invertible primary rotation (`encode.rs:82-104`). Correct the inverse to the configured target primaries, defaulting to sRGB.
- **Auto Profile tail — invert ONLY the #550 per-channel curve:** that curve is monotone, per-image, 1D-LUT-invertible (forced non-decreasing at fit, `curve.rs:247-271`; fit per image, `fit_display.rs:113-266`). **The separate residual 3D ColorLut has no analytic inverse and is many-to-one near gamut corners (`lut.rs:197/281/303`) — disable it** (`MAPLE_AUTO_LUT_STRENGTH=0` / `MAPLE_DISABLE_AUTO_LUT`, `lut.rs:143-164`). **The legacy `apply.rs` cross-channel matrix + Oklab corrections are not per-channel invertible at all (`apply.rs:45-110`) — the design must guarantee the display-space #550 fit is the only Auto Profile path in play.** And the inverse must **snapshot and re-apply the exact cached `(curve)` for that specific image**, since the curve is fit to the JPEG (which lacks the patch).

**Required accuracy:** good enough that under a ±2-stop exposure / WB push the patch tracks the surrounding plate to within the slider-matrix harness budget (mean ΔE2000 well under the loose 25 ceiling, ideally a few ΔE) **in midtones**, with shadows/highlights degrading gracefully (monotone, band-free, finite). Because exposure is a scene-linear multiply *before* AgX, small chroma errors get non-linearly amplified by the sigmoid — chroma accuracy on near-saturated pixels matters most, and is exactly where the inverse is weakest, so keep saturated content off the gamut wall. **The bar is monotonic, band-free behavior under a push on in-gamut midtone content, not bit-exact reconstruction.** And the inverse must match the CPU *and* GPU LUTs bit-for-bit (`raw-gpu/src/agx.rs:43`).

### c. Mask storage: new local-adjustment kind; where do baked pixels live?

**Model removal as a new entry in the existing `Vec<LocalAdjustment>` channel — but note the new entry needs a new raster-mask + patch-ref representation, not the existing parametric `Mask` (§3a).** Add a 3rd variant (e.g. `Removal { region, patch_ref }`) — geometry can stay vector for composite *bounds*, but the actual coverage is a raster mask (new) and the pixel payload is a `patch_ref` content hash, **not** XMP-embedded. Extend `mask_from_json`/`mask_to_json` (`wire.rs:56-109`). Non-breaking: the format is still pre-UI/pre-user-sidecar (`mod.rs:20-22`) and unknown adjustment keys pass through silently (`wire.rs:288-294`). Masks serialize as compact JSON in one attribute `papp:LocalAdjustments` (`wire.rs:1-23`) — which is unsuited to a megapixel raster, hence the raster mask + patch live by reference, not inline.

**Baked patch pixels go in `.maple/inpaint/<hash>.f16`, never in XMP.** XMP carries only user-authored scalar/vector adjustments (`xmp-canonical-format.md:279-285`). The precedent is exact: pano writes `.maple/panos/<jobId>.png` content-addressed then re-indexes (`pano-stitch.ts:281-321`); thumbs/previews live under `.maple/{thumbs,previews}/` (`db/schema.ts:222-224`). The XMP holds only a reference (hash/relpath).

**Three concrete gaps to budget:** (1) neither Swift nor TS models `local_adjustments` today and codegen excludes it (`schema/mod.rs:5-6`) — both need hand-written encode/decode. (2) The **Swift XMP writer has no passthrough preservation** (`XMPSerialization.swift:110-174`, `XMPSidecarStore.swift:115-125` rebuilds the whole sidecar from the model) — it will silently delete anything it doesn't model. Storing removal inside the modeled `papp:LocalAdjustments` JSON attribute is safe; migrating to nested `crs:` RDF is **not** (Swift would drop it). (3) The tile/zoom path **rejects** active local_adjustments (`tile/mod.rs:158-176`, verified) because mask coords are full-image-normalized and "a padded crop cannot reproduce them without offset plumbing" — and a fixed-resolution raster patch is *strictly harder* than a parametric mask here. Decide explicitly whether removal is allowed at zoom (needs window-offset + patch-resample plumbing) or rejected like other local adjustments.

**Forward/back-compat of the `Removal` wire variant (verified against `wire.rs`).** The current decoder is *not* tolerant: `decode_local_adjustments` does `arr.iter().map(layer_from_json).collect::<Result<Vec,_>>()` (`wire.rs:33`), so the `collect` **short-circuits on the first `Err`** — and `mask_from_json` returns `Err("unknown mask type")` for anything but `linear`/`radial` (`wire.rs:107`, pinned by the `decode_unknown_mask_type_errors` test, `wire.rs:275-279`). Consequence: an **older build that opens a sidecar containing a `removal` layer loses *every* local adjustment in the file** (whole-attribute parse failure), and because the Swift writer has no passthrough, a subsequent save makes the loss permanent. The doc comment at `wire.rs:8-13` confirms **no user-authored sidecars exist yet** (pre-UI) — that is the open migration window. Plan, in order:

1. **Expand reads before writes — land a tolerant reader first, into the open window.** Replace the `collect()` with a per-element loop: a *known-but-malformed* element still errors (keep the corruption guard, `decode_missing_mask_field_errors`), but an element with an **unrecognized `mask.type` or a `schema` newer than known** is *skipped and its raw `serde_json::Value` preserved* into an `unknown_layers: Vec<Value>` on the model. This converts "lose everything" into "ignore the unknown." Rewrite `decode_unknown_mask_type_errors` — it currently encodes the hazard.
2. **Preserve-on-write, attribute-local (not full XMP passthrough).** Both writers re-emit the preserved `unknown_layers` verbatim, closing the Swift destructive-save hole during the interim before every platform models `removal`.
3. **Discriminate additively.** Removal is a new layer with `"schema": 2` (or `"kind":"removal"`) at the *layer* level (old `layer_from_json` ignores keys beyond `mask`/`adjustments`) + a new `"mask":{"type":"raster",…}`. Pixels never inline: the layer carries `patch_ref` (blake3 → `.maple/inpaint/<hash>.f16`), `model_version`, and the **bake-grade snapshot** (WB/EV/tone the inverse was computed against — needed for the §4 inverse-composite *and* for deterministic regeneration). Deterministic LaMa means a missing `.maple` asset regenerates from the XMP alone.
4. **Defensive (builds already in the field, pre-step-1):** the removal layer may also carry a parseable **no-op decoy mask** (degenerate `radial`, zero radii) so a pre-tolerant reader parses it as harmless instead of failing the array — belt-and-suspenders; the clean path is reader-first sequencing into the `wire.rs:8-13` window.
5. **Tests gate it:** new→old→new preserves the removal via preserve-unknown; old-format sidecars round-trip byte-identical; unknown-mask-type no longer fails the whole array.

### d. Cache invalidation: re-inpaint only on request; grade reuse without re-running the model?

**Reuse the `#950` baked-vs-stripped discipline.** Maple already implements "re-render the grade but don't re-run the expensive stage": the in-memory decode cache keys on the *baked (stripped) model*, not sidecar mtime (`RenderActor+DecodedCache.swift:208-216, 324-334`). A grade-only autosave bumps mtime but the baked model is unchanged, so the decode stays a **hit**. (The memory-file claim "sidecar-mtime bump invalidates the decoded cache" is *denied* post-#950 for the in-memory buffer; it remains true only for the rendered-preview disk cache, which keys directly on `sidecarMtime`, `RenderedPreviewCache.swift:137`.)

The patch is a "KEPT/baked" artifact. Its key includes source + mask + model version but **explicitly omits grade fields and sidecarMtime**. A mask edit or explicit re-inpaint changes the key → model re-runs. A grade edit changes neither the key nor the patch — it only re-runs the stripped develop chain over the cached patch. Re-inpaint happens **only on explicit user request or mask edit**, never on a slider tick.

One caveat: because the cached patch is a function of *that specific inverse-mapped bake*, and because the inverse must re-apply the per-image Auto Profile curve (§3b), the cache key must thread the **per-image Auto Profile curve identity** (per-image, `fit_display.rs:113-266`) and the source content hash (reuse `decode_cache::from_bytes` blake3, `decode_cache.rs:95-114`), so a re-export or curve change rebuilds it.

### e. On-disk bit-depth/colorspace of the cached patch?

**fp16 Rec.2020, scene-linear, RGBA (alpha = feather), stored as raw bytes `.maple/inpaint/<patchKey>.f16` — never JPEG.** fp16 matches the established Apple working buffer (`CIFormat.RGBAh`, `ImageEditPipeline.swift:1237`) and the raw-core FFI interchange (`fp16.rs`). It is half the size of fp32, identical size to 16-bit int, but **preserves linear-light headroom above 1.0 and below 0** that 16-bit-int normalization would clip — which the scene-referred invariant forbids.

Footprint: a typical 2000×1500 (3.0 MP) removal region RGBA = **22.9 MB** fp16 (vs 45.8 MB fp32). Worst-case full-frame 100MP RGBA ≈ **815 MB** fp16 (vs 1.63 GB fp32). Copy `RenderedPreviewCache`'s 500MB LRU sweep, scoped strictly to `.maple/inpaint/`. **Do NOT** reuse `DecodedBufferCache`/`RenderedPreviewCache` — both store lossy 8-bit display-referred sRGB JPEG (`DecodedBufferCache.swift:88-91`, `RenderedPreviewCache.swift:105-108`), the wrong color state, which would silently destroy the wide-gamut headroom the bake-once design exists to preserve.

### f. Core ML / model packaging: bundled vs downloaded, versioning, footprint?

**Downloaded on first use, SHA-256-pinned, never committed — copy the `PanoProvisioner` + `models.toml` pattern, but generalize it (it is not a drop-in).** Models pin `file/sha256/size/source/license` in `models.toml` (Rust, `include_str!`) mirrored in `PanoProvisionManifest.swift` (Apple); `ModelDir::resolve` verifies size then streamed SHA-256 before any session, hard-erroring on mismatch with no "load anyway" path (`models.rs:163-226`). `PanoProvisioner` (actor) downloads via `URLSession` with progress, verifies, atomically installs, idempotently, and re-verifies the dylib at load (`PanoProvisioner+Integrity.swift:24-41`). Path resolution is 3-tier (UserDefaults → env var → app-support default).

**Required changes (the "build, not drop-in" part):** the manifest is a *fixed* `{aliked, lightglue}` struct (`models.rs:163-171`) mirrored as a hardcoded 2-model Swift array (`PanoProvisionManifest.swift:55-70`) — generalize to a `Vec<ModelEntry>` / keyed map on both Rust and Swift sides; add a session wrapper per model; and add a **new inference FFI** (the only ML FFI today is the monolithic `maple_pano_stitch`, `pano_apple.rs`). **ORT gotchas inherited:** macOS dylib must stay **≥1.23** (1.22 aborts at process exit on macOS — the documented reason for the 1.23.2 pin); keep the `libloading` preflight (ORT `load-dynamic` panics on missing dylib); iOS is **static-link 1.22.0** — a *different* version the new models must tolerate. **Memory:** ORT memory-pattern + CPU arena pre-allocate gigabytes (pano disables both, saving ~4GB); a diffusion U-Net needs the same knobs + tiling. **Footprint:** pano models ≈59MB + dylib ≈10MB ≈ 70MB on disk; LaMa adds ~50–100MB, SD1.5-inpaint palettized ~1.3GB.

### g. Segmentation model for auto people-removal — separate or reuse?

**Must be added — nothing reusable exists.** The only on-device ML is pano keypoint matching (ALIKED+LightGlue, not segmentation) and *server-side* face detection (SCRFD+ArcFace via `onnxruntime-node`, a **separate runtime** from the pano ORT, `api/src/index.ts:30`; bbox not masks). The describe stage (qwen2.5-vl) is **server-side and caption-only** — an HTTP call to local Ollama (`describe-providers/ollama.ts:71`) returning a text blob, no coordinates, no masks. It cannot produce a removal mask.

**The right answer is to NOT ship a segmentation model on Apple at all for v1:** use Apple Vision's `VNGeneratePersonInstanceMaskRequest` / `VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest` (zero download, ANE). Reserve a bundled ONNX segmentation model (BiRefNet MIT) for the web path, where Vision doesn't exist. The mask is geometry — it survives the inverse-tone-map unchanged; only the inpainted color needs inverse-AgX.

### h. WASM/web target: v1 or Apple-only?

**Apple-only v1; defer web explicitly.** Web has zero ML deps (no `onnxruntime-web`/`transformers.js`/tfjs/mediapipe), `raw-wasm` depends only on `raw-core` + optional `raw-gpu` (no `ort`/pano/ml feature; `maple-pano`'s own comment notes "ort has no wasm story here"), and the team already documented ORT-on-WASM as an unstarted later milestone (`maple-pano/Cargo.toml:28-31`). The WASM heap is already tight — the render worker uses `take_rgb`/`take_fp16_rgba` specifically to hold one frame at a time on 100MP RAWs (`raw-pipeline.worker.ts:206-289`); a diffusion model's weights+activations on top would blow the ~4GB budget. Web would also be Maple's *first* persistent per-asset render-derived cache (Apple's #950 decode cache is Apple-only; web re-renders per edit via `render_bytes`), so the web bake-once path is new ground, not a port — and, per the verifier, web has **no ONNX runtime to reuse at all**, so "reuse rather than build from scratch" is *false* on web. File a tracking ticket referencing `maple-pano/Cargo.toml:28-31` as precedent, and make the deferral explicit in product scope (parity is normally a merge gate).

## 4. Recommended pipeline placement

Mapping the brief's 7-step flow onto real files:

| # | Brief step | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Generate mask | **Apple side**: `VNGenerate{Person,Foreground}InstanceMaskRequest` (auto) or canvas paint (manual). New Swift code in `MapleApp`. No raw-core involvement. |
| 2 | Crop mask-bbox tile, sRGB-encode | New native crate (sibling to `maple-pano`). Crop the display-referred preview; LaMa/MI-GAN consume 8-bit sRGB. |
| 3 | Run inference (LaMa/MI-GAN ONNX) | Same crate, via `ort` + CoreML EP — copy `OrtRuntime::preflight` (`models.rs:328-401`), the `lightglue.rs` session pattern, and `PanoProvisioner`. **New inference FFI** required (not `maple_pano_stitch`); **CoreML op coverage for these models is unvalidated** — CPU-ORT is the correctness floor. |
| 4 | **Inverse-map** display sRGB → *pre-user-grade* scene-linear Rec.2020 f32 | **raw-core, new module** `view::agx_inverse` (natural home near `view/auto_profile/bake.rs`, which already samples the forward curve into a grid). The inverse must run **all the way back to the cached-buffer color state**, not just past AgX (see "Critical wiring" below). Reverse order: u8→linear (exact) → un-curve via the **#550 per-channel curve inverse only**, 3D LUT + legacy Oklab corrections disabled → inverse sRGB OETF (exact) → **target→Rec.2020 matrix (sRGB default, P3 if configured), exact** → inverse-AgX (reverse-sigmoid LUT on max-channel ratio + inset/outset, **clamped domain + shoulder splice**) → **inverse of the live grade: `tone_curves` (monotone LUT) → user `exposure`/scene_tone_controls (`2^-EV`) → `white_balance` delta (`wb_gains(decoded)/wb_gains(bake)`)**. Do **not** invert DCP or AE — they are baked upstream of the seam (`develop/mod.rs:332`/`:409`) and the patch lands in the same post-DCP/post-AE space as sensor data. Snapshot and re-apply the image's exact per-image Auto Profile curve + bake-time grade. Must match the GPU/WGSL LUT bit-for-bit. **This is the load-bearing novel code with no precedent — accurate in midtones, bounded-error in shadows/highlights, never trust boundary chroma.** |
| 5 | Render the model input with creative/spatial stages **off** | The bake preview is rendered through only `WB → exposure → tone → AgX` — **not** vibrance/saturation/HSL/clarity/texture/dehaze/vignette. Those are nonlinear/spatial and not cleanly per-pixel invertible, so they are *excluded from the bake* and instead **ride the live chain** (step 7), applying to the patch exactly as to its neighbours. The inpainter still sees correct tonal context; the flat synthetic patch picks up creative polish live. Resample the patch to the target buffer resolution in **linear light**. |
| 6 | **Composite** `out = lerp(orig, patch, mask_w)` at the **pre-user-grade seam** | **New raw-core composite**, injected into the cached scene-linear buffer at the seam **immediately before `white_balance`, after AE/DCP** — `develop/mod.rs:411` (full develop) and `scene_linear_chain.rs:146` (per-tick), *not* the `local_adjustments` slot (which is post-grade — see "Critical wiring"). Asserts `SceneLinearRec2020`. Uses a **new raster-mask + patch carrier** (the existing parametric `Mask`/`PartialAdjustments` cannot express pixel replacement — §3a). Must short-circuit bit-identically when no patch present. |
| 7 | Grade + view transform | Unchanged — `white_balance` → … → `view::agx::apply` (`render/mod.rs:216`) and the whole tail run over the composited buffer exactly as for sensor pixels. The patch re-grades on every slider move because it now sits *upstream* of the grade. |

**Critical wiring detail for the live editor (corrected):** the patch must enter where **sensor data enters its user grade** — the seam *before* `white_balance`, after AE/DCP. Both chains have this seam at the same place: `develop/mod.rs:411` and `scene_linear_chain.rs:146`. The `local_adjustments` slot (`develop/mod.rs:455` / `scene_linear_chain.rs:214`) is **after** WB/exposure/tone and is therefore the wrong place: a patch injected there is *frozen at bake-time grade* — move a slider and the whole image shifts except the patch (a seam). Equivalently, injecting *post-grade* inverse-AgX pixels at a *pre-grade* point double-applies the grade. The fix is to invert the live grade (step 4) so the patch arrives in pre-grade space, then let the live chain re-apply it once (step 7). DCP and AE are baked once upstream of the seam and are never re-applied live, so they are **not** inverted. Baking the patch to the cache resolution avoids per-tick resampling.

**FFI surface:** both platforms already consume scene-linear via `render_bytes_scene_linear*` (WASM `raw-wasm/src/lib.rs:362,422`) and `maple_render_*_scene_linear_f32` (Apple `PipelineRenderer.swift:458,509`), and `render_from_scene_linear` (`render/mod.rs:320`) runs only the view tail. The "inject synthetic-raw patch before the view transform" primitive is **one shared raw-core function** both call. Keep the inference itself out of raw-core/raw-wasm (native-only sibling crate) so the WASM build stays model-free — but note this means raw-core must expose a hook the native crate feeds patches into, i.e. a **new raw-core↔ML data edge** that does not exist today.

## 5. The determinism / bake-once decision

**Bake-once is correct. The evidence is unambiguous and the case is stronger than "diffusion is stochastic."** Five independent sources confirm on-device inference is non-reproducible:

- PyTorch: "results may not be reproducible between CPU and GPU executions, even when using identical seeds."
- HF Diffusers: same-seed reproducibility "not *guaranteed* even with an identical seed."
- Apple's own `ml-stable-diffusion`: "Completely reproducible results are not guaranteed."
- Root cause: fp16 non-associativity — different GPU kernels/shapes accumulate in different orders → different values.
- Maple's own pano path confirms it in-house: macOS runs CPU-ORT, iOS runs CoreML/ANE (reduced precision), and **iOS CoreML output is explicitly unvalidated** ("pending M6-E", `lightglue.rs:110`). No bit-exactness is claimed across EPs.

**The feed-forward eraser changes the calculus in Maple's favor.** For diffusion, the bake is *load-bearing* — you cannot re-derive the user's chosen generation across a chip generation or a Core ML point-update; the XMP must reference an opaque cached blob. For **deterministic LaMa/MI-GAN**, the output is a pure function of `(source_content_hash, mask_hash, model_version)`. The bake becomes a **recomputable performance cache**: cache it to avoid re-running on every open, but if it's evicted or the model upgrades, regenerate deterministically from the XMP-recorded mask. This is strictly more aligned with "XMP is the contract, pixels are derived" — the patch is reproducible from the sidecar, not a frozen artifact. **Recommendation: ship deterministic so the cache is an optimization, not a correctness requirement.** Either way the cache key and on-disk format are identical (§3d, §3e); determinism only changes whether eviction is recoverable.

One caveat regardless: the cached patch is a function of *that specific inverse-mapped bake*, so the cache key must thread the per-image Auto Profile curve identity (`fit_display.rs:113-266`) and the source content hash (`decode_cache.rs:95-114`), so a re-export or curve change rebuilds it.

## 6. Risks, unknowns, and what to prototype first

**Riskiest assumptions, in order (re-ranked after verification):**

1. **AgX inverse accuracy under exposure/WB pushes — confirmed as *the* load-bearing unknown, and the verifier sharpened *why*.** The inverse is analytically sound in midtones but (a) **non-injective and ill-conditioned at the AgX toe/shoulder** — sub-LSB display steps map to >1-stop scene-linear error (`agx.rs:100-105`), (b) **non-invertible at the Oklab gamut walls** (twice: `agx.rs:144`, `encode.rs:88`), and (c) dependent on inverting **only** the #550 per-channel curve while keeping the residual 3D LUT and legacy Oklab corrections out of play (`lut.rs`, `apply.rs:45-110`). Because exposure is a pre-AgX scene-linear multiply, small recovered-chroma errors get non-linearly amplified by the sigmoid — an inverse that looks perfect at the baked grade can diverge at +2 EV, especially on near-saturated content. **Measure this; do not assume it.** And the inverse must match the CPU and GPU LUTs bit-for-bit (`raw-gpu/src/agx.rs:43`).

2. **Compositing resolution / tiling.** The patch is fixed-resolution; preview (half-res), refine (full-res), and deep-zoom tile buffers all differ. Resampling *pixels* (not a scalar mask) risks seams and gamut error if done non-linearly. The tile path already hard-errors on local_adjustments because mask coords are full-image-normalized (`tile/mod.rs:158-176`) — a fixed-resolution raster patch is *strictly harder*; deep zoom needs explicit offset/scale + patch-resample plumbing or must be disabled while a patch is active.

3. **New composite construct (not a reuse).** The existing parametric mask + scalar-delta machinery cannot replace pixels (§3a, verifier-refuted). The new raster-mask + patch-carrier + `inpaint_composite` stage is net-new raw-core code — budget it as such, not as an extension of `local_adjustments`.

4. **ML plumbing is new, not a drop-in.** raw-core has no ML edge, the FFI is a monolithic stitch call, the manifest is a fixed 2-model struct, and CoreML op coverage for LaMa/SAM is unvalidated (verifier). The provisioning *pattern* copies cleanly; the *wiring* does not.

5. **Swift XMP passthrough gap.** Any removal data not in the modeled `papp:LocalAdjustments` JSON attribute is silently deleted on the next Swift edit (`XMPSerialization.swift:110-174`). Keep removal strictly inside the modeled JSON; do not migrate to nested `crs:` RDF without fixing Swift passthrough first.

6. **`.maple/inpaint/` GC must never touch originals.** Per the standing invariant ("originals are sacred", no asset-deletion code), the LRU sweep must be provably scoped to `.maple/inpaint/` with a test that it cannot escape — and because the *diffusion* patch is non-reproducible, it must NOT be swept like a regenerable thumb (a carve-out from the "derived data is reproducible" assumption at `xmp-canonical-format.md:283`).

**Smallest spike that de-risks the whole design (do this first, before any model or UX work):** A headless raw-core round-trip test in `maple-cli`. Take a known scene-linear patch → forward-render through the **full live grade + view tail** (`WB → exposure → tone → AgX`, creative/spatial stages off — the bake-preview path) → quantize to 8-bit sRGB → invert back to **pre-grade** scene-linear via the new `agx_inverse` module (**#550 curve only; 3D LUT + legacy Oklab corrections disabled; per-image curve snapshotted; target=sRGB**, then inverse `tone → exposure → WB-delta`) → composite at the **pre-user-grade seam** (`scene_linear_chain.rs:146`, before `white_balance`) → re-render under exposure −2/0/+2 EV and temp ±1000K → diff the patch region against the un-inverted reference with `compare_images.py` (ΔE2000). **The test must prove the patch re-grades coherently under slider moves** (the seam-on-slider failure), not just that AgX round-trips at the bake grade. **Stratify the metric by tonal zone** — report midtone, shadow, and highlight ΔE separately, since the inverse is only trustworthy in midtones. If mid-tone patches track within a few ΔE under ±2-stop / ±1000K pushes and shoulder/toe pixels degrade gracefully (no banding, finite highlights via the linear splice), the architecture holds. This spike needs *no* ML model, *no* Swift, *no* UI — just the inverse math and the composite at the seam, gated by the existing parity harness. It validates the single hardest, most novel piece — the **full inverse (AgX + live grade)**, not AgX alone — in isolation.

## 8. Verification notes

Three adversarial verifiers each checked one riskiest claim against the code. **All three returned "partial"** — the architecture is sound, but each claim was qualified. Confirmed-vs-qualified ledger:

### 8.1 AgX invertibility — **PARTIAL** (`verify:agx-invertible`)
**Confirmed:** the forward chain is ordered exactly as the design assumes (`render/mod.rs:216` agx → `:241` Oklab `rec2020_to_srgb` → `:247` gamma → `:258` auto_profile → `:279` quantize) and a scene-linear injection point already exists (`render_from_scene_linear`, `render/mod.rs:320`; `skip_agx` FFI, `scene_linear_chain.rs:229`). Inset/outset matrices, the monotone sigmoid LUT interior, the display matrices and sRGB OETF are all invertible.
**Qualified / corrected:**
- AgX is **non-injective and ill-conditioned at the extremes** — clamp `[-10,+6.5]` EV (`agx.rs:100-105`, `agx_coeffs.rs:22-23`; `agx.rs:315`); 1 LSB near toe/shoulder can exceed a full scene-linear stop. *(Draft called this merely "lossy"; it is a multiplicative-stop-scale error — downgraded to bounded-error, midtone-only.)*
- Oklab gamut compression is **genuinely many-to-one** (`oklab_gamut.rs:43`, `agx_hue_restoration.rs:95`) — never reconstruct from boundary-saturated chroma.
- Auto Profile is **two artifacts**: the monotone #550 per-channel curve (invertible, `curve.rs:247`, `fit_display.rs:259-264`) **plus a separate residual 3D ColorLut** (`lut.rs:197/281/303`, applied `render/mod.rs:154`) that is **not closed-form invertible**, **plus** a legacy `apply.rs` cross-channel + Oklab path that is **not per-channel invertible at all** (`apply.rs:45-110`). Invert through the #550 curve only; disable the LUT; guarantee the legacy path is off. *(Draft under-stated the LUT and missed the legacy path.)*
- The curve is **per-image, fit to the JPEG** (which lacks the patch, `fit_display.rs:67-265`) — must snapshot + re-apply the exact per-image artifacts.
- **Default encode is Rec.2020→sRGB**, not P3 (`encode.rs:28-34`); P3 is a separate, also-invertible rotation (`encode.rs:82-104`). *(Draft said P3 — corrected.)*
- **No inverse exists in the codebase**; it is net-new and must match the `raw-gpu`/WGSL LUT bit-for-bit (`raw-gpu/src/agx.rs:43`).
**Net:** true under five constraints (midtone-only, #550-curve-only, per-image snapshot, bounded shadow/highlight error, never trust boundary chroma); false as an unqualified claim. The Phase-0 spike now stratifies ΔE by tonal zone to test exactly this.

### 8.2 Clean compositing — **PARTIAL** (`verify:composite-clean`)
**Confirmed (the load-bearing half):** the buffer type, color space, and timing all exist as needed. `Image { Vec<[f32;3]>, SceneLinearRec2020, unbounded }` (`image.rs:17-19, 47-53`); chain enters scene-linear after DCP (`develop/mod.rs:332`) and AgX is last, gated by `skip_agx` (`scene_linear_chain.rs:229`). The pre-view-transform insertion point is real.
**Refuted (the "reuse the mask machinery" half):** the existing local-adjustment system **cannot** composite synthetic pixels —
- `Mask` is a closed parametric enum (`Linear`/`Radial` only, `mod.rs:107-130`); no raster mask; brush masks are *rejected* today (`wire.rs:277`).
- `apply_pixel` only *multiplies* (`exp2(w·ev)`, `local_adjustments/mod.rs:76-98`) — no pixel-replace; `out=mix(scene,patch,mask)` is inexpressible.
- masks are resolution-agnostic by design (`mod.rs:24-27`); a fixed-resolution raster patch must be resampled to every buffer size and the tile path already rejects local adjustments for exactly this reason (`tile/mod.rs:158-176`).
**Net:** reuse the *buffer type*; build a **genuinely new construct** for the mask + patch carrier + composite stage. The draft already concluded "new stage" — §3a and §4 are sharpened to state plainly that the *machinery* is not reusable.

### 8.3 ML-infra reuse — **PARTIAL** (`verify:ml-reuse`)
**Confirmed:** a real vendored `ort` with a real CoreML EP (`vendor/ort/.../coreml.rs`), wired live (`lightglue.rs:161-169`, `aliked.rs`); a real SHA-256-pinned download/verify/atomic-install provisioner with load-time re-verify (`models.rs:54-226`, `PanoProvisioner.swift:74-211`, `PanoProvisioner+Integrity.swift:24-41`). Good prior art to copy.
**Qualified / corrected (downgraded the draft's "~80% reuse / wholesale"):**
- The ML stack lives in **`maple-pano`, not `raw-core`** (raw-core has zero ort/onnx) — but the scene-linear composite must be in raw-core, so a **new raw-core↔ML edge** is required.
- **Web has no runtime at all** (`raw-wasm` no ort feature) — "reuse rather than build from scratch" is **false on web**.
- The FFI is a **monolithic `maple_pano_stitch`** (`pano_apple.rs`), not a general inference API — new FFI required.
- The manifest is a **fixed 2-model struct** (`models.rs:163-171`) + hardcoded Swift array (`PanoProvisionManifest.swift:55-70`) — must be generalized.
- There are already **three separate ML/provision stacks** (Rust ort / API `onnxruntime-node` faces `api/src/index.ts:30` / antelopev2 `face-models-bootstrap.ts`) — pattern is "each feature ships its own," evidence for copyability and against a shared system.
- **CoreML-EP reuse is unvalidated for new op-sets** (iOS-only, macOS stays CPU, "unvalidated on-device — pending M6-E", `lightglue.rs:110`); iOS ORT 1.22 static vs macOS 1.23.2 dylib skew.
**Net:** copy the provisioning + EP-registration *pattern*; **build** the raw-core↔ML edge, a generic inference FFI, a multi-model manifest, and per-model CoreML validation. Reuse estimate corrected from ~80% to roughly ~50% (pattern reuse, new plumbing).

### 8.4 Items confirmed without qualification
The draft's other file:line references checked out as stated: scene-linear buffer shape and unbounded gamut (`image.rs:17-19, 47-53`); composite slot and short-circuit (`develop/mod.rs:455-457`, `local_adjustments/mod.rs:31-34`); the `#950` baked-vs-stripped decode-cache discipline (`RenderActor+DecodedCache.swift:208-216`); rendered-preview disk cache keying on `sidecarMtime` (`RenderedPreviewCache.swift:137`); cache stores being lossy 8-bit sRGB JPEG (`DecodedBufferCache.swift:88-91`, `RenderedPreviewCache.swift:105-108`); tile-path local-adjustment rejection (`tile/mod.rs:158-176`); pano artifact precedent (`pano-stitch.ts:281-321`, `db/schema.ts:222-224`); XMP-reference-only storage (`xmp-canonical-format.md:279-285`); Swift XMP no-passthrough (`XMPSerialization.swift:110-174`); web ORT-on-WASM deferral (`maple-pano/Cargo.toml:28-31`); and the describe stage being server-side caption-only (`describe-providers/ollama.ts:71`).
